Google has many different domains and including all domains (obsolete, disabled etc.) then it turns out 4000+ domains such as .ru / .com / .org / .net etc.

National (.ru .de .jp etc.) are domains that are used in different countries. For example if a person creates a website for his store which is located in Germany then he uses the national domain .de

Generic old (.com .org .net .info .biz...) are old domains that have existed for a long time. They are used most often — 53–58% of websites.

Generic new (.site .online .shop .tech...) these domains were created in the last 10 years. They are less popular than others and they are very rarely seen on popular websites because they mainly use domains such as .com / .org.

Brand (.google .apple .microsoft...) these domains are used by companies/organizations/popular persons. They cannot be registered by an ordinary person only by an official person. There are about 450+ such domains.

Sponsored (.gov .edu .museum .coop...) these domains also cannot be registered just like that. They can only be obtained after passing verification and they are issued as privileges that are very valuable.

Reserved (.example .test .localhost...) they are created mainly for tests and code. They also cannot be registered. They are created for documentation and similar mainly used in tests.

Obsolete (.nato .ddn .su) this is the most important section. There are many different interesting things with these "dead" domains and there are even working oldest sites. One of them is .nato. It was removed because the NATO network became obsolete and they switched to a new one namely .int. The very first .nato domain has been gone for 30 years — it was completely deleted. Even the USSR had an official domain which was the .su domain and this domain is still ALIVE and it has NOT been completely deleted. But you can no longer register a new site on it. There is even a live site with this domain — zavod.su. The domain for this site was bought for 5+ years in advance and in about 6 years it will disappear forever and sites with .su will be completely deleted.

Author's comment about .su: I went to sites like zavod.su and demos.su and I saw old sites that were created 1989 - 2000 and most likely in the next 5 years these sites may lose their host and may disappear and almost all sites with the .su domain will disappear forever. I hope someone remembers these sites and captures them before deletion
